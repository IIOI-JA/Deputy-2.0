Overview

One platform, two depths of engagement

Deputy is offered in two clearly distinct product levels. Both use the same platform and the same therapeutic origin — but they differ fundamentally in how they are used. Neither level requires the psychologist to compromise on their method, their language, or their clinical judgement. The choice is not about which is better, but about how much presence a given moment calls for. Many practices will use both, depending on the client and the phase of treatment.

Deputy Scale & Deputy
Interactive Scale

Same platform. Same therapeutic origin. Two fundamentally different ways to work.

Deputy Scale keeps the psychologist's proven method exactly as it is, delivered through a single, well-produced channel. Deputy Interactive Scale takes that same method and lets it respond, within limits the psychologist sets in advance. Together, they describe not two products, but two depths of the same therapeutic relationship.

Deputy Scale

A method many psychologists already know

The psychologist creates a therapeutic protocol or exercise, and makes it available to the client as audio content. This is the same practice many psychologists already follow informally — recording a guided exercise, a breathing sequence, or a reflection prompt for a client to use between sessions. Deputy simply gives that habit a consistent, professional home. Nothing about the underlying clinical content needs to change to make use of it.

Deputy Scale does not change the therapeutic logic — it professionalises and simplifies how it is delivered.

Deputy Interactive Scale

The interactive evolution

Deputy Interactive Scale is the interactive evolution of Deputy Scale — and corresponds to Deputy's current core product. Where Deputy Scale ends the exchange at delivery, Deputy Interactive Scale keeps it open. The client can respond, and Deputy can respond back — always inside limits the psychologist has already set. It is the same therapeutic origin, simply extended one step further into the client's week.

The same platform. The same therapeutic origin. A deeper form of engagement.
Deputy Interactive Scale

Beyond a static protocol

The client doesn't just receive a static protocol. Within the framework defined by the psychologist, Deputy can respond to input and accompany the therapeutic process interactively between sessions. This does not mean the system improvises. Every possible direction the conversation can take has already been anticipated and approved by the psychologist beforehand, so nothing the client encounters falls outside what was agreed.

Strategic Logic

Why two levels, not two products.

It is a question of sequencing, not of choosing sides. Most practices will move through both stages as their comfort with the platform grows. What matters is that the starting point costs nothing in clinical integrity, and the next step costs nothing in rework.

Strategic logic

Two entry depths, not two competing products

The two variants are not competing products, but two entry depths into the same Deputy platform. A psychologist new to digital delivery can begin with Deputy Scale and feel no disruption to how they already work. There is no obligation to ever move further, and no penalty for staying at this level indefinitely.

A

Deputy Scale is the entry point.

B

Deputy Interactive Scale is where they can grow.

This sequencing matters because trust in a new tool is built gradually, not assumed. By the time a psychologist considers Deputy Interactive Scale, they already know exactly how their content sounds and behaves on the platform.

Product path

One path: Scale to Interactive Scale

A psychologist can start with Deputy Scale: existing exercises and protocols are professionally recorded and made available through Deputy. Later, the same content can — if desired — be transformed into an interactive Deputy structure. Nothing already recorded needs to be redone; the same audio protocol becomes the foundation for the interactive version, extended rather than replaced.
